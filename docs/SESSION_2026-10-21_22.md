# Session summary — 2026-10-21 / 22: arm safety, the camera, and the first guarded runs on the arm

This work was operator-directed, outside claude-auto. Each step on the physical arm was taken on the operator's
explicit "go".

## 1. arm_guard: a last-line filter in front of every motor command (2026-10-21)

The operator asked for something that guarantees the IK can never break the arm.
- `packages/arm_guard` patches `motorbridge` in every Python env that can reach the motors (`rebot`, `lerobot`).
  Every command, from any caller, is checked first:
  - joint window, step size against the measured position, and stopping-distance speed;
  - MIT gains and feed-forward caps;
  - keep-out against the table, the base and the upper arm;
  - motor health, and a startup check that the arm is folded and the encoders agree (J1 unwrapped by whole
    turns).
- A violating command is never sent. The arm holds and the guard latches; it never cuts power.
- The config is checksummed and approved.
- The guard suite has 203 tests, including fuzzing, a replay of every logged real run, and mutation checks.
- Docs: [ARM_GUARD.md](motion/ARM_GUARD.md).

## 2. The trajectory runner (2026-10-21)

The operator's proven controller (`~/rebot_setup/demo_move.py`) became guard-aware:
- it runs the guard pre-flight before enabling;
- on a guard fault it holds and the operator decides;
- it can run `crackvision.joint_trajectory/1` files, through `run-trajectory.sh` (rehearse, check, run).

Two guard bugs were found by rehearsal in simulation and fixed. Runbook: [FIRST_GUARDED_RUN.md](motion/FIRST_GUARDED_RUN.md).
The code snapshot is in [evidence/operator/rebot_setup_runner_2026-10-22](../evidence/operator/rebot_setup_runner_2026-10-22/README.md).

## 3. The D405 camera (2026-10-22)

- **Research.** DigiKey 99ACXD is the D405, camera only. It has a **USB 3 Micro-B** socket (not USB-C); the newest
  D405 firmware is 5.17.0.10; the 6.8 kernel needs no DKMS patch.
- **Software.** `scripts/camera.py`:
  - `check`: CAM-01 evidence;
  - `record --video`: lossless and validated, plus an MP4;
  - `snap`: capture, then the full crack pipeline;
  - `live`: the model on every frame, in a browser.
- **Real camera.**
  - Serial 260522273107, firmware 5.15.1.55, USB 3.2, 30 fps with no dropped frames.
  - 10 s video test.
  - Live crack view: 30 fps video, the model at 17 passes a second.
  - The model was made 3.8× faster with identical output: batched tiles, weights loaded once.
- Docs: [D405_SETUP.md](camera/D405_SETUP.md), [D405_FIRST_CONNECTION.md](camera/D405_FIRST_CONNECTION.md).
- Test prints: two Wikimedia Commons crack photos (not from any training dataset), `data/crack_targets/`.

## 4. The first guarded trajectories on the physical arm (2026-10-22)

`smoke_raise.json` at half speed: fold → shoulder −0.6 / elbow −0.9 rad → fold.
- **Shown in simulation first:** a guarded rehearsal, rendered in MuJoCo (`tools/render_arm_run.py`).
- **Two safe stops at the fold first:**
  - J1 had lost its turn count after a 24 V cycle. The operator confirmed it faced forward and J1 was re-referenced.
  - A dropped register reply refused J3's enable. Fixed: the guard now retries.
- **Run 1 (kd 1): completed.** Tracking within 0.023 rad, no guard fault. The operator saw a **wobble**: J2/J3
  rang at 3.3 Hz (damping ratio about 0.1, re-excited by gearbox stick-slip, worse at low speed).
- **Run 2, A/B with J2/J3 kd 4: wobble down 61 % (J2) / 48 % (J3)**, again no guard fault.

Evidence: [evidence/operator/first-guarded-run-2026-10-22](../evidence/operator/first-guarded-run-2026-10-22/README.md).
