# Operator note — first arm bring-up evidence and next-session preparation (Oct 10, 2026)

## What was done

- **The operator brought up the DIY B601-DM kit by hand, read-only on the software side.**
  - Each motor's CAN ID was set in DMTool, one motor at a time: motor N at `0x0N`, feedback at `0x1N`.
  - The zero was set in MotorBridge Studio in Seeed's folded "sit-down" pose and verified after a power cycle.
  - A readback-only sign check ran with every motor disabled: 2766 samples. Every joint's direction matches
    the URDF. The gripper opens towards negative motor angles.
  - At the end, every joint rested within 0.05 rad of zero.
  - No script enabled a motor, and nothing moved under power.
- **That evidence is now in the repo**, filed with `claude-auto evidence add`:
  - `evidence/operator/MOT-09/` has the raw log, scans, register dump and sign-check data, unmodified.
  - It also has a scoped summary and a machine-readable facts file.
  - `evidence/operator/HOST-04/` holds the cited USB ID of the arm's USB/CAN adapter (`2e88:4603`).
  - The summary says what the evidence does **not** establish: no e-stop test, no powered motion, no limit
    verification, no commissioning. `config/robot/commissioning.yaml` is unchanged and still uncommissioned.
- **The plan was updated for the first powered session.**
  - MOT-09 (arm bring-up commissioning) went from a draft to a concrete operator procedure: e-stop wiring and
    tests, power-on scan and rest check, a first low-gain move of joint1 only, a dry rehearsal of the gated
    executor against the vendor driver, a readback-only range survey, and then the commissioning record.
  - HOST-04 (udev rule for the adapter) now cites the real USB ID and can be unblocked.
  - Two new software cards cover what a read-only survey of the vendor stack found:
    - **MOT-05.7.** The gated executor cannot read the vendor driver's joint states, because of a QoS
      mismatch.
    - **MOT-11.** An automated check that the vendor driver's port, IDs, joint order, directions and gripper
      sign match the bring-up evidence.
- **The vendor driver is not ready.** The read-only survey found:
  - **Driver will not start.** The vendor ROS driver needs an older version of the vendor motor SDK than the
    one checked out. Two Python dependencies are also missing for the system interpreter.
  - **Default "safe park" pose is unsafe on this unit.** It would push the forearm into its folded rest.
  - **Launch energises every motor.** Starting the driver powers all of them immediately.

  None of this was changed: the vendor workspace is read-only for this project. The proposed fixes are left
  for the operator.

## Why it was done

REQ-INT-2 requires separately gated hardware commissioning backed by operator-sourced evidence. The bring-up
facts (IDs, zero, signs) are the foundation for every later physical step. They needed to be recorded with a
clear scope before anything moves. The next attended session also needed a step-by-step, gated path from
power-on to the first powered motion, with the vendor-side blockers known in advance.

## How it moves the project forward

- The physical track is still at **0/6 accepted**. Nothing physical was completed, and nothing is claimed.
- MOT-09 is no longer a draft. HOST-04 has its cited prerequisite. The executor's last known defect against the
  real driver has a fix card, MOT-05.7.
- An operator-side script for the first joint1 move is being prepared outside the repo, in `~/rebot_setup/`,
  for the operator to review. It only runs in an attended session.
