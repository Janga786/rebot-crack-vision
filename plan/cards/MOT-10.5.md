Everything runs offline or on the headless mock stack. The sweep and the scene smoke test only make IK/FK queries and planning requests, so nothing moves.

1. `bash scripts/ros/build_ws.sh`.
2. Inside env_ros.sh with the overlay sourced: `ros2 run crackvision_motion survey_to_scene --survey config/scene/workcell_survey.yaml --out config/scene/scene.yaml --emit-verify-config config/motion/measured_placement_verify.yaml --emit-view-config config/motion/measured_view_verify.yaml`. Exit 2 means the survey is invalid. In that case stop and report; do not edit the survey.
3. Record `sha256sum config/scene/scene.yaml` and then run the acceptance checks yourself in order: scene-matches-survey, commissioning-ready, scripts/ros/test_scene.sh, then the two measured verification runs. Run the sweeps in the background and watch their progress logs.
4. Compare the as-placed pose with config/motion/specimen_placement.yaml. Leave specimen_placement.yaml untouched: it stays the nominal recommendation, and the measured pose now lives in scene.yaml.
5. Update SCENE.md §4 and REACHABILITY.md as the criteria require. Be honest about failures: a failed verification is a valid outcome that must be reported, never worked around.

The GPU is shared. If MoveIt is degraded by GPU contention, follow docs/ARCHITECTURE.md §9 and do not kill other processes.
